Neem Quick Help

Recordings are saved automatically as MP4 to Movies › Neem and listed on the home screen.

Capture area: use the Capture row to record a whole display, one app window, or a custom region you drag out.

Cursor zoom: when it is on, Neem eases into a close-up around your pointer when you settle on something, then eases back out. Shortcuts while recording: ⌘⇧Z zoom in · ⌘⇧X zoom out · ⌘⇧P pause · ⌘⇧S stop.

Permissions (macOS): System Settings › Privacy & Security › allow Neem for Screen Recording, Camera and Microphone.

“Neem is damaged / cannot be opened”? That is macOS Gatekeeper, not a broken download. In Terminal run:
xattr -cr /Applications/Neem.app
then reopen the app.
